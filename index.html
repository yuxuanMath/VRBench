What does the overall score mean?＋

The leaderboard averages the composite scores of 50 tasks. Each task score in this average combines its stages by weight. Each stage keeps 10% of its task-stage score. The remaining 90% is scaled by the product of its tracking and collision scores, each divided by 100.

A stage keeps only the 10% share if tracking drops below 40, collision drops below 30, or even one sampled arm–environment penetration reaches 60 mm (50 mm beyond the 10 mm tolerance). Each task specifies which intended contacts to exclude.

How does the offline score relate to closed-loop success?＋

The offline score measures the quality of a plan predicted from the initial views. The closed-loop success rate measures how often the task is completed in simulation with a shared executor. The executor can recover from some planning errors or fail to carry out a sound plan.

How can I evaluate another model?＋

Configure your model’s API, then run vrbench predict, vrbench solve, and vrbench score on the dataset. You can start with one task or score the full suite.
